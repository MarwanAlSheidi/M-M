# Canned Light Tuna in Sunflower Oil — pricing decision brief

All prices per kg of finished product. OMR amounts use the fixed peg 1 USD = 0.3845 OMR. Every input is a
published benchmark, not a verified purchase or sale (see FINDINGS.md).

## Recommendation
Pursue the UAE export channel. It is the only wholesale channel with meaningful margin above the floor at the
benchmark raw-material price, and at the top of its price range it stays above the floor across the whole
plausible raw-material range. That robustness depends on selling near the top of the UAE range (see below).
Do not pursue Oman wholesale (the Oman import benchmark).

## The number
- Floor price (last viable sell): 1.832 OMR/kg (~4.76 USD/kg)
- Target price (30% margin): 2.224 OMR/kg
- Ceiling price (45% margin): 2.831 OMR/kg

The floor is the same for every channel because it is set by cost and the minimum margin. Below it, the
product is not viable at any margin floor.

## Why UAE export
At the benchmark frozen skipjack price of 1.40 USD/kg (Oman landed, Tridge May 2026), UAE export clears the
floor by +24% at the top of its benchmark range (5.91 USD/kg = 2.272 OMR/kg). At that price it does not breach
the floor even at skipjack 1.80 USD/kg, well above the current benchmark (headroom +9.2% at 1.70, +5.1% at 1.80).

The +24% is measured against the top of the UAE range. Lower in the range the margin is much thinner:

| UAE selling price | Headroom at skipjack 1.40 | Floor breached from skipjack |
|---|---|---|
| 5.91 USD/kg (top) | +24.0% | about 1.94 USD/kg (outside the range) |
| 5.00 USD/kg (middle) | +5.0% | about 1.52 USD/kg |
| 4.80 USD/kg (bottom) | +0.8% | about 1.43 USD/kg |

## Why not the other channels
- Oman wholesale (Oman import benchmark): below cost even at the top of its range (3.89 USD/kg = 1.496
  OMR/kg, against a unit cost of 1.557). It only reaches the floor if skipjack falls to about 1.00 USD/kg, well
  below the plausible range.
- MENA export: clears the floor by +0.3% at benchmark, breaches at skipjack 1.45 USD/kg. Inside the noise band.
- Oman retail: a shelf price, not a channel. Ignore.

## What would change this answer
1. Actual skipjack purchase price (currently a benchmark, not a PO). A higher price shrinks margin. UAE export
   survives any price in the range only if we sell near the top of the UAE range; at the middle of that range
   it breaches from about 1.52 USD/kg, and at the bottom from about 1.43.
2. Actual selling price achieved inside the UAE export range (1.846 – 2.272 OMR/kg). Top of range gives +24%
   headroom, bottom gives +0.8%. The channel works; your position within it decides the business case.
3. Product spec changes (fish content, can size, species). These are not modeled. A 50% fish-content product
   would move the floor down significantly.
